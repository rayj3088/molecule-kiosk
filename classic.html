A fantasy machine. The assembler is a black box on purpose, so nothing here shows how atoms become things.

Ingredient lists are simplified ideas, not recipes. Tap vials to pick your own mix. If it matches a listed item, the box makes it. If not, you get a gray lump.

Loose hydrogen, oxygen and nitrogen atoms pair up at once, so a real version would stock stable compounds such as water, carbon dioxide and ammonia.
