# Molecule Kiosk

A fantasy molecule vending machine.

- `index.html`: wireframe 3D prototype. Drag to spin, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  - Two racks: 30 elements, or 30 biological building blocks (20 amino acids, 4 DNA letters, fuel and salts) feeding a cell-free ribosome core.
  - Sealed boxes (Design/AI, Feed, Core, Sense, Form, Sign, Power) you can probe and reverse engineer.
  - `.molx` recipe files, a difficulty ladder (L0 simple substance to L3 muscle tissue), contained replication for grown foods, and a Sign check that refuses copies of the machine.
  - Nature tab: why copying beats placing atoms one at a time, and a scoreboard of the ways nature cheats.
- `classic.html`: the original flat kiosk.

Everything behind the glass is speculation. Recipes and box rules are simplified toy rules, not real chemistry.
